# DistroKid & Streaming Platform Strategy (2026)

## Platform Payouts & Strategy
*   **Amazon Music:** Highly lucrative for ambient/sleep channels due to Alexa smart speaker looping.
*   **Apple Music:** Pays 2-3x more than Spotify. Offers a +10% bonus for Spatial Audio (Dolby Atmos).
*   **Spotify:** Has a 1,000 stream minimum threshold per track before paying out. **CRITICAL WARNING:** Spotify's audio compression algorithm aggressively folds stereo separation, which destroys the neurological effect of **binaural beats** (QuantumFrequencies). Do NOT rely on Spotify for binaural beats; prioritize Apple Music/Tidal.
*   **Tidal / Deezer:** Tidal pays the highest rate (~$0.013/stream). Deezer's artist-centric model pays ~$0.007/stream.

## Distributor Selection: DistroKid
*   **Best for Volume:** DistroKid's "Musician Plus" tier ($39.99/yr) supports unlimited uploads and two artist slots (perfect for running `Pure Static` and `Sleepy Cloud Lullabies` simultaneously).
*   **Automation:** DistroKid **does not have a public API**. Unofficial wrappers like `distrogo` are brittle and cannot upload. The proven 2026 method is headless browser automation (Playwright/Puppeteer) to log in, bypass 2FA via persistent profile, and submit the form.
*   **Asset Quality:** Upload 16-bit 44.1kHz WAV files and 3000x3000px artwork.
